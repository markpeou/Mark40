The destinations

Saigon, then Siem Reap

Saigon

Motorbikes, iced coffee and street food at all hours. Scooter food tours, rooftop bars and Bùi Viện walking street.

Siem Reap

A relaxed river town beside Angkor, the world's largest temple complex. Sunrise at Angkor Wat, jungle roots swallowing Ta Prohm, then pool, massages and Pub Street.

Weather
Wet season: hot, with short afternoon storms. The jungle is at its greenest.
Money
Dong in Vietnam, US dollars in Cambodia.
Language
Mark speaks Khmer. Siem Reap is his turf.

What to expect

The week, loosely

Angkor Wat is the one must-do. Otherwise: pool, markets, shopping, whatever you like. Dinner together every night.
