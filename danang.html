The destinations

Saigon, then Da Nang

Saigon

Motorbikes, iced coffee and street food at all hours. Scooter food tours, rooftop bars and Bùi Viện walking street.

Da Nang and Hoi An

Vietnam's beach city: long sandy beaches, seafood straight from the tank, and the Marble Mountains next door. Hoi An's lantern-lit old town is 45 minutes away.

Weather
Dry season in Da Nang: hot, sunny, calm warm sea.
Money
Dong. Cards work almost everywhere.
Language
Mark doesn't speak Vietnamese. That's half the fun.

What to expect
